Starting points you can drop in and adjust: whole pages, single panels, and looks.

| Kind | Is | Find it |
|---|---|---|
| Layout starters | One sensor-free panel; each widget asks for a sensor | **Add › Layouts** |
| Panel presets | One panel already bound to sensors (samples) | **Add › Panels** |
| Panels | Panels you saved | **Add › Panels** (under My panels), **Library › Panels** |
| Widget looks | One widget's look | Style tab, **Start from a saved look**, **Library › Widget looks** |
| Themes, saved themes and template themes | A whole theme for a screen or a page | **Theme**: **Browse themes** and **Saved and template themes** |
| Colors | One part of a theme | The **Colors** section of **Fine-tune** in **Theme** |

## Widget styles

A widget style is how every widget is drawn (a skin in saved files). Pick one on the **Theme** row of the theme card in Theme, for a screen or a page, or per widget in its Style tab. Picking one is a single undo step and keeps the colors, corners, background and fine-tune.

:::generated presets.appearance

## Saved themes and palettes

**Save as my theme** in Theme, or **+ Save current look** in **Saved and template themes**, keeps the level's colors, theme, corners, background and fine-tune. **Browse themes** and **Saved and template themes** apply a theme, a saved theme or a template's theme to a screen or a page; it brings its background unless **Keep my background** is on. On a page a theme brings its look and fine-tune only. Rename, duplicate or delete a saved theme from its **⋯** there.

**+ Save current colors as a palette** in the Colors picker keeps the 19 color tokens as a palette of your own. See [Themes](themes).
