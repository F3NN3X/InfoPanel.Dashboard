# Day Planner

Calendar events on a time rail, with the weather.

## What it shows

Today's events on a rail with a now line, over a band for the temperature and rain, vertical or horizontal. Small zones
get a summary card (the next event) and a dense view of their own.

## Data it needs

The calendar links set under Settings › Connections (served by the server, not sensor keys), and the Yr weather plugin,
`plugins.yr-weather-plugin.*` (read for the weather band). It draws without sensor keys too.

## Options

- **Look ahead**: 6, 12, 24 or 36 hours.
- **Direction**: Auto, Vertical or Horizontal.
- **Weather band**, **Weather location** and **Temperature unit**.
- Units & format: the clock format.
