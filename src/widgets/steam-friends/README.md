# Steam Friends

Friends by status, in-game first.

## What it shows

Your Steam friends as a list or a table, colour-coded by status: in-game, online, away, busy and offline. Small zones get a
summary card and a dense view of their own. It sends to the Dynamic Island.

## Data it needs

The Steam API plugin's social container, `plugins.InfoPanel.SteamAPI.SteamAPI-Social.*` (it owns the prefix).

## Options

- **View**: List or Table; **In-game only**, **Last online** and **Counts row**.
- The five status colours (the theme's Series 1, OK, Warning, Critical and Series 2 unless set).
- **Sort**: Status, Name or Plugin order.
