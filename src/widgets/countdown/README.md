# Countdown

Days until an event.

## What it shows

Counts down to an event, or up from it: years, months, days and live hours, minutes and seconds, as inline text, tiles or
one big number. Small zones get a summary card and a dense view of their own.

## Data it needs

No sensor keys: the event is set in the options.

## Options

- **View**: Inline, Tiles or Big number; **Event name**, **Date** and **Time of day** (empty = an all-day event).
- **Count**: Auto, Until or Since; **Breakdown**: Auto, Days, Years or Live.
- **Show the date**; Units & format: the clock format.
