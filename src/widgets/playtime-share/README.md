# Playtime Share

Two weeks of play, split by game.

## What it shows

How the last two weeks of play split between games: one bar, segments sized by share, with sessions and library totals.
Small zones get a summary card and a dense view of their own.

## Data it needs

The Steam API plugin's library container, `plugins.InfoPanel.SteamAPI.SteamAPI-Library.*` (read, not owned).

## Options

- **Slice 1** to **Slice 6** colours (the theme's Series 1 to 6 unless set).
