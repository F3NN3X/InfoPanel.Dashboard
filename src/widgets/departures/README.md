# Departures

The next departures from your stop.

## What it shows

Public transport departures with time, line and destination, the station name and a status line. Small zones get a summary
card and a dense view of their own.

## Data it needs

The VBZ plugin, `plugins.InfoPanel.VBZ.*`: `station_name`, `status`, and per departure `dep_<n>_line`, `dep_<n>_dest` and
`dep_<n>_time`.

## Options

- **Status line**.
