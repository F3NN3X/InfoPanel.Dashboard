# Stream Control

OBS scenes, live timer, mic mute and replay.

## What it shows

OBS scene buttons, a live tile with the timer and viewers, mic mute with its level and replay save. Connect OBS under
Settings › Connections. Small zones get a summary card and a dense view of their own.

## Data it needs

The server's OBS connection (`useExtras().obs`, acting through `src/lib/obs-control.ts`), not sensor keys.

## Options

- **Show mic** and **Show replay**.
