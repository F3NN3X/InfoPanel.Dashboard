One model, three placements
01
Quick barThe settings people change most sit right above the canvas. Pin any setting with the pin icon to add it. Inspired by Canva and Google Slides.
02
One-column inspectorContent, Style and Data tabs with accordion sections. Everything reads top to bottom and never reflows sideways. Inspired by Figma and Elementor.
03
Changed and searchSearch runs across all tabs. Changed shows only what you customised. A dot resets one setting, the section icon resets a group.
04
Placement follows spaceAt 1100px and up, the inspector docks to the side. Below that it becomes a floating panel you open when needed, which also replaces the bottom dock for bar displays. On phones it becomes a bottom sheet with a tool strip, like CapCut or Lightroom.
Try it: switch viewports above, double-click a widget, and drag the sheet handle on Phone.
